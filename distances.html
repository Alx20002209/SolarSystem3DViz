Planets and asteroids use the Sun as their reference centre. Moons use their parent planet. The reference orbit is the nominal orbital distance used to calculate the display factor, not the changing distance at this instant. One scene unit is a virtual 3D coordinate unit, not a pixel or a physical metre.

Formula: displayed relative vector = real relative vector / km per scene unit. For moons, add the parent's displayed position afterward. Relative direction and radial variation are preserved. The Sun sits at the origin, so its distance factor is not applicable. Illustrative body sizes use separate factors; rings, glow and atmosphere are excluded from radius columns.
